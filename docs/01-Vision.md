# 01 — Vision

## Vision

Speech is useful only when another person can understand it.

For people with dysarthria, the intention behind a sentence may be perfectly clear in their mind while the sound reaching another person, or a speech-recognition system, is difficult to interpret.

PEEXH exists to reduce that gap.

## Product Vision

PEEXH is a personalized voice communication agent that:
1. listens to the user's speech;
2. interprets likely intended phrases;
3. decides how much confirmation is needed;
4. lets the user confirm or correct the result;
5. communicates only the confirmed message;
6. learns from corrections to improve future interactions.

## One-Line Product Definition

> **PEEXH is a personalized communication aid that learns to better understand the speech of people with dysarthria.**

## What PEEXH Is Not

PEEXH is not:
- a speech-therapy app;
- a medical diagnosis tool;
- a replacement for clinicians;
- an autonomous medical system;
- a general-purpose assistant;
- a meeting bot;
- a translation platform;
- an emergency dispatch system.

## Long-Term Direction

If V1 proves useful, the same core interaction may later support:
- more languages;
- more speech conditions;
- additional communication contexts;
- deeper personalization;
- device-native deployments;
- private/on-device components.

Those possibilities must not dilute V1.
