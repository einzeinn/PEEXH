# 03 — Business

## Purpose

This document describes product value without forcing PEEXH into premature monetization.

## User Value

PEEXH reduces communication friction for people whose speech may be difficult for listeners or conventional STT to understand.

The value proposition is straightforward:

> **Speak naturally. Confirm your meaning. Be understood.**

## Potential Beneficiaries

Primary:
- individuals with dysarthria.

Secondary ecosystem:
- families;
- caregivers;
- accessibility organizations;
- rehabilitation programs;
- speech-language professionals as a complementary tool;
- assisted-living environments.

## Business Value

PEEXH may create value through:
- improved day-to-day communication;
- reduced repeated speech attempts;
- more personalized assistive technology;
- reusable user-specific communication memory;
- applicability across multiple everyday contexts.

## Possible Future Models

Not part of V1:
- free individual tier;
- paid personalization/storage tier;
- organization-supported access;
- clinic/rehabilitation partnerships;
- accessibility grants;
- device licensing.

## Product Constraint

Business expansion must not undermine user control, privacy, or accessibility.

PEEXH must not create artificial friction around essential communication simply to force upgrades.
